What QuoteFlow is not

  • Not a rebuild of your website.
  • Not automatic quoting or price calculation — pricing stays with you.
  • Not a monthly SaaS subscription.
  • Not a template sold on its own.

What we do not promise

We do not guarantee revenue, more jobs won, or a particular response time. What is delivered is the setup described above, tested and handed over. Whether it earns its cost depends on your volume and how your team uses it, and we would rather say that up front than after you have paid.

How it works

  1. Walkthrough. You show us how requests come in today and what happens to them. If your current setup already works, we will say so rather than sell you a package.
  2. Scope in writing. We confirm exactly what will be built, at which price, before any payment.
  3. Build and test. We build it and run test requests through the whole path.
  4. Handover. You own everything that was built. There is no lock-in and no recurring fee.

How this relates to our referral work. QuoteFlow is Oakline Partners' own service, built and delivered by us and paid for by the client. It is separate from our referral and affiliate activity, where we introduce businesses to third-party software vendors and are paid by the vendor. We do not implement or support the vendor software we refer, and buying QuoteFlow is never a condition of a referral — or the reverse.
