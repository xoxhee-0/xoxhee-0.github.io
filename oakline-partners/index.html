We connect service businesses with software that fits how they actually work.

Oakline Partners is an independent referral partner run by one person — Lee Sohee — not an agency or a reseller. We research small and mid-sized service businesses, look at how they handle scheduling, dispatch, quoting and customer follow-up, and introduce the ones with a clear gap to established software vendors through those vendors' official partner programs.

What we do

  1. Research one business at a time. Its service area, how it takes bookings, whether quotes and follow-ups live in one place or are scattered across tools — before we contact anyone.
  2. Reach out individually. Each message is written for that specific business and sent directly to it. We do not run bulk campaigns and we do not buy contact lists.
  3. Introduce only on interest. A business is referred to a vendor only after it has told us it wants to look at the product. No interest, no referral.
  4. Hand over to the vendor. Demos, pricing and contracts are handled by the vendor's own team. We do not sell, resell, implement or support the software.

Where we focus

Field service and home service operators in the United States and Canada — the kind of business where a missed call or a quote that never gets followed up is a job lost to a competitor.

How we work with vendors

We join official partner and referral programs and follow each program's terms. We keep referral volume low on purpose: a vendor's sales team should receive businesses that are genuinely considering a change, not a list of names. Where a program measures lead quality, keeping that number healthy matters more to us than submitting volume.
